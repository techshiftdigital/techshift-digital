CAGR Calculator

Calculate compound annual growth rate from starting value, ending value and number of years.

Enter your values and select calculate.

How this tool works

CAGR = (ending value ÷ starting value)^(1 ÷ years) − 1. For 10,000 growing to 20,000 in five years, CAGR is about 14.87%.

Important limitations

Both values and the period must be positive. CAGR smooths growth and does not describe year-to-year volatility or cash flows.
