What is the CAGR Calculator?

Calculate compound annual growth rate between a starting value and ending value. Use it for a quick, transparent result, then review the method and boundaries below before relying on the answer.

Formula

CAGR = (ending value ÷ beginning value)^(1 ÷ years) − 1.

The calculator keeps full precision during the calculation and rounds the displayed result for readability.

Practical example

$10,000 growing to $20,000 over 5 years has a CAGR of approximately 14.87%.

How to use this in Excel

Place the inputs in the cells referenced below, enter the formula, and apply the appropriate number format.

=(B2/A2)^(1/C2)-1

Review units and cell references before copying the formula down a worksheet.

Common mistakes and limitations

  • Using zero or negative endpoint values.
  • Treating CAGR as the arithmetic average of annual returns.
  • Ignoring additional deposits or withdrawals.

Important limitation: CAGR smooths all intermediate volatility into one annualized rate and does not show the path, risk or cash flows between endpoints.
