What is the Date Difference Calculator?

Find the elapsed years, months, weeks and days between two calendar dates. Use it for a quick, transparent result, then review the method and boundaries below before relying on the answer.

How it works

Elapsed time is calculated from the earlier date to the later date using calendar boundaries; the total-day figure is the millisecond difference divided by 86,400,000.

The calculator keeps full precision during the calculation and rounds the displayed result for readability.

Practical example

From 1 January 2026 to 15 January 2026 is 14 elapsed days, or exactly 2 weeks.

How to use this in Excel

Place the inputs in the cells referenced below, enter the formula, and apply the appropriate number format.

=DATEDIF(A2,B2,"y")&" years, "&DATEDIF(A2,B2,"ym")&" months, "&DATEDIF(A2,B2,"md")&" days"

Review units and cell references before copying the formula down a worksheet.

Common mistakes and limitations

  • Mixing inclusive and exclusive counting.
  • Entering timestamps when only calendar dates are intended.
  • Assuming every month contains 30 days.

Important limitation: Calendar months have different lengths. Inclusive counting adds one day and may be preferable for bookings or leave records.
