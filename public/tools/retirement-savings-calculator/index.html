What is the Retirement Savings Calculator?

Project a retirement balance from current savings and recurring monthly contributions. Use it for a quick, transparent result, then review the method and boundaries below before relying on the answer.

How it works

Projected balance combines compound growth of the current balance with the future value of monthly contributions.

The calculator keeps full precision during the calculation and rounds the displayed result for readability.

Practical example

$50,000 plus $750 monthly for 20 years at 7% grows to approximately $594,000 before fees, tax and inflation.

How to use this in Excel

Place the inputs in the cells referenced below, enter the formula, and apply the appropriate number format.

=FV(B2/12,C2*12,-D2,-A2)

Review units and cell references before copying the formula down a worksheet.

Common mistakes and limitations

  • Ignoring inflation.
  • Assuming one constant return every year.
  • Treating the projected balance as a guaranteed outcome.

Important limitation: The result is a nominal projection, not a retirement plan. Returns, inflation, taxes, fees and contribution changes matter.
