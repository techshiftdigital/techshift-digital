What is the Discount Calculator?

Calculate the amount saved and final price after a percentage discount. Use it for a quick, transparent result, then review the method and boundaries below before relying on the answer.

Formula

Discount amount = original price × discount rate; final price = original price − discount amount.

The calculator keeps full precision during the calculation and rounds the displayed result for readability.

Practical example

A 20% discount on $250 saves $50 and produces a final price of $200.

How to use this in Excel

Place the inputs in the cells referenced below, enter the formula, and apply the appropriate number format.

=A2*(1-B2)

Review units and cell references before copying the formula down a worksheet.

Common mistakes and limitations

  • Entering 20 instead of 20% in a spreadsheet formula.
  • Applying sales tax before or after the discount without checking local rules.
  • Adding successive discounts together instead of applying them sequentially.

Important limitation: Tax, delivery fees and stacked promotions are not included unless already reflected in the entered price or rate.
