  1. Enter the source values carefully.

Formula

The method used by this tool is principal × (1 + rate ÷ n)^(n × years). Keep the source values and units with the result so another person can reproduce the calculation.

Worked example

1,000 at 5% for 3 years monthly → 1,161.47. This example demonstrates the sequence; your result depends on the values and options you enter.

Excel guidance

Use this workbook approach: =FV(B2/C2,C2*D2,0,-A2). Format percentage results as Percentage, dates as dates, and financial values with the correct currency. Avoid rounding intermediate steps.

Frequently asked questions

Does this tool upload my data?

No. The calculation runs locally in your browser.

Can I use the result in professional work?

Use it as a checked working estimate. Confirm source data, units, dates and business rules before relying on it.

Why might Excel show a different result?

Differences usually come from rounding, date systems, percentage formatting or different assumptions.

Can I bookmark this page?

Yes. The URL is stable, but values are not saved after you leave the page.
