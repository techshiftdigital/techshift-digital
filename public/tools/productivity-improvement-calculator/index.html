What is the Productivity Improvement Calculator?

Estimate time saved and annual value from a faster workflow. Use it for a quick, transparent result, then review the method and boundaries below before relying on the answer.

Formula

Time saved per year = old minutes − new minutes, multiplied by occurrences; value saved = hours saved × hourly cost.

The calculator keeps full precision during the calculation and rounds the displayed result for readability.

Practical example

Saving 10 minutes on a task performed 20 times per week saves about 173.3 hours over 52 weeks.

How to use this in Excel

Place the inputs in the cells referenced below, enter the formula, and apply the appropriate number format.

=((A2-B2)*C2*D2)/60

Review units and cell references before copying the formula down a worksheet.

Common mistakes and limitations

  • Counting theoretical time that cannot be redeployed.
  • Ignoring implementation and training cost.
  • Using inconsistent weekly and annual frequencies.

Important limitation: The model assumes saved time is usable and the task volume remains constant. Quality changes and implementation costs are excluded.
