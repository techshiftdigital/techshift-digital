Productivity Improvement Calculator

Estimate hours saved, productivity improvement and annual labor savings.

Enter your values and select Calculate.

How it works

Time saved per task = original time − new time. Annual savings = time saved × task volume × hourly cost.

Important limitations

Assumes consistent task volume and that saved time has an equivalent labor value; savings are not automatically cash savings.
