What is the Meeting Cost Calculator?

Estimate the labour cost of a meeting from participant rates and duration. Use it for a quick, transparent result, then review the method and boundaries below before relying on the answer.

Formula

Meeting cost = participants × average hourly cost × duration in hours.

The calculator keeps full precision during the calculation and rounds the displayed result for readability.

Practical example

Eight people at $40 per hour in a 90-minute meeting cost an estimated $480.

How to use this in Excel

Place the inputs in the cells referenced below, enter the formula, and apply the appropriate number format.

=A2*B2*(C2/60)

Review units and cell references before copying the formula down a worksheet.

Common mistakes and limitations

  • Entering minutes as hours.
  • Using take-home pay instead of employer cost.
  • Ignoring recurring frequency.

Important limitation: Salary cost is not the same as total employment cost. The estimate excludes preparation, follow-up and opportunity cost.
