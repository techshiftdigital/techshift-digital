Salary Calculator

Convert salary between annual, monthly, weekly and hourly pay, and calculate a salary increase.

Enter your values and select Calculate.

How it works

Annual salary uses 52 weeks, 12 months and the specified hours per week. The raise is applied to the annual equivalent.

Important limitations

Gross amounts only; taxes, benefits, overtime and unpaid leave are excluded.
