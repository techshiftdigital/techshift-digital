What is the SIP Investment Calculator?

Estimate the future value of recurring monthly investments at an assumed return. Use it for a quick, transparent result, then review the method and boundaries below before relying on the answer.

Formula

Future value of monthly contributions = payment × [((1+r)^n − 1) ÷ r] × (1+r) for beginning-of-period deposits.

The calculator keeps full precision during the calculation and rounds the displayed result for readability.

Practical example

Investing $500 monthly for 10 years at 8% annual return grows to roughly $91,473 with beginning-of-month deposits.

How to use this in Excel

Place the inputs in the cells referenced below, enter the formula, and apply the appropriate number format.

=FV(B2/12,C2*12,-A2,0,1)

Review units and cell references before copying the formula down a worksheet.

Common mistakes and limitations

  • Treating the assumed return as guaranteed.
  • Using annual contributions with a monthly rate.
  • Ignoring whether deposits occur at the beginning or end of the month.

Important limitation: Market returns are not constant or guaranteed. Taxes, fees, missed contributions and inflation are excluded.
