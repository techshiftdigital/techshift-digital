Electricity Cost Calculator

Estimate electricity usage and cost from power, daily hours and tariff.

Enter your values and select Calculate.

How it works

kWh = watts ÷ 1,000 × hours per day × days. Cost = kWh × tariff.

Important limitations

Actual bills may include fixed charges, taxes, tiers and varying consumption.
