What is the Electricity Cost Calculator?

Estimate energy use and electricity cost for an appliance. Use it for a quick, transparent result, then review the method and boundaries below before relying on the answer.

Formula

Energy (kWh) = watts ÷ 1000 × hours per day × days; cost = kWh × tariff.

The calculator keeps full precision during the calculation and rounds the displayed result for readability.

Practical example

A 1,500 W heater used 4 hours daily for 30 days consumes 180 kWh. At $0.20/kWh, it costs $36.

How to use this in Excel

Place the inputs in the cells referenced below, enter the formula, and apply the appropriate number format.

=(A2/1000)*B2*C2*D2

Review units and cell references before copying the formula down a worksheet.

Common mistakes and limitations

  • Using watts as kilowatts.
  • Forgetting daily usage frequency.
  • Applying one tariff to tiered pricing.

Important limitation: Actual bills may include tiered rates, taxes, demand charges and standby consumption.
