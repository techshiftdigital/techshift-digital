What is the GST / VAT Calculator?

Add or remove GST/VAT and show the tax amount and net price. Use it for a quick, transparent result, then review the method and boundaries below before relying on the answer.

Formula

Tax-inclusive total = net price × (1 + rate); tax extracted from a gross price = gross × rate ÷ (1 + rate).

The calculator keeps full precision during the calculation and rounds the displayed result for readability.

Practical example

At 15% tax, a net price of $200 produces $30 tax and a $230 gross total.

How to use this in Excel

Place the inputs in the cells referenced below, enter the formula, and apply the appropriate number format.

Add tax: =A2*(1+B2); extract tax: =A2*B2/(1+B2)

Review units and cell references before copying the formula down a worksheet.

Common mistakes and limitations

  • Subtracting the rate directly to remove included tax.
  • Mixing percentage values and decimals.
  • Ignoring item-level rounding rules.

Important limitation: Tax rules, exemptions, rounding and invoice requirements vary by jurisdiction. This tool performs arithmetic only.
