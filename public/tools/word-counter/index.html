Word and character counter

Count whitespace-separated words and characters in your text.

Enter your values, then select the action above.

What the counts mean

Words are counted as groups of non-whitespace characters. Characters include spaces and line breaks. The tool also reports characters excluding whitespace.

Important boundaries

Word counts may differ from document editors, especially for hyphenation, punctuation and languages that do not separate words with spaces. Maximum input: 200,000 characters.
