What is the Password Strength Checker?

Estimate password strength from length and character variety without sending the password anywhere. Use it for a quick, transparent result, then review the method and boundaries below before relying on the answer.

How it works

This estimator scores length and character-class diversity; it is a heuristic, not a breach database or cryptographic audit.

The calculator keeps full precision during the calculation and rounds the displayed result for readability.

Practical example

A unique 18-character passphrase is generally more resilient to guessing than P@ssw0rd, despite the latter mixing character types.

Common mistakes and limitations

  • Relying on substitutions such as @ for a.
  • Ignoring reused or breached credentials.
  • Treating a visual score as a security guarantee.

Important limitation: The score cannot prove a password is safe and does not check whether it has appeared in known breaches. Do not enter a real sensitive password on an untrusted device.
