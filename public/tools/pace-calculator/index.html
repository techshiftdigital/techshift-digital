What is the Pace Calculator?

Calculate running pace and average speed from distance and elapsed time. Use it for a quick, transparent result, then review the method and boundaries below before relying on the answer.

Formula

Pace per kilometre = total time ÷ distance; average speed = distance ÷ time in hours.

The calculator keeps full precision during the calculation and rounds the displayed result for readability.

Practical example

Completing 10 km in 50 minutes gives a pace of 5:00 per km and an average speed of 12 km/h.

How to use this in Excel

Place the inputs in the cells referenced below, enter the formula, and apply the appropriate number format.

Minutes per km: =B2/A2 when B2 is total minutes and A2 is distance

Review units and cell references before copying the formula down a worksheet.

Common mistakes and limitations

  • Entering hours as minutes.
  • Mixing miles and kilometres.
  • Treating average pace as every split pace.

Important limitation: Average pace does not show hills, stops or splits. GPS and measured-course errors can affect the result.
