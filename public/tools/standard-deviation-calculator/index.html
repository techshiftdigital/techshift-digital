What is the Standard Deviation Calculator?

Calculate population and sample variance and standard deviation. Use it for a quick, transparent result, then review the method and boundaries below before relying on the answer.

Formula

Population variance = Σ(x−μ)² ÷ N; sample variance uses n−1 in the denominator.

The calculator keeps full precision during the calculation and rounds the displayed result for readability.

Practical example

For 2, 4, 4, 4, 5, 5, 7 and 9, the population standard deviation is 2.

How to use this in Excel

Place the inputs in the cells referenced below, enter the formula, and apply the appropriate number format.

Population: =STDEV.P(A2:A9); Sample: =STDEV.S(A2:A9)

Review units and cell references before copying the formula down a worksheet.

Common mistakes and limitations

  • Using population formulas for sample data.
  • Removing outliers without justification.
  • Comparing standard deviations measured in different units.

Important limitation: Standard deviation is sensitive to outliers and is most interpretable with the distribution and units.
