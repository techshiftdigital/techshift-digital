Percentage Calculator

Calculate percent of a number, what percentage one value is of another, and the original number before a percentage change.

Enter your values and select calculate.

How this tool works

For 15% of 250, multiply 250 × 15 ÷ 100 = 37.5. This differs from percentage change, which compares a new amount with its starting amount.

Important limitations

For reverse calculations, a 100% decrease leaves zero and cannot be reversed to a unique starting value.
