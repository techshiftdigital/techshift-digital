What is the Percentage Calculator?

Calculate a percentage of a number, determine what percentage one number is of another, or apply a percentage change. Use it for a quick, transparent result, then review the method and boundaries below before relying on the answer.

Formula

x% of y = (x ÷ 100) × y; a as a percentage of b = (a ÷ b) × 100.

The calculator keeps full precision during the calculation and rounds the displayed result for readability.

Practical example

15% of 240 is 36. Also, 36 is 15% of 240.

How to use this in Excel

Place the inputs in the cells referenced below, enter the formula, and apply the appropriate number format.

=A2*B2 for a decimal rate in A2; =A2/B2 to find a proportion

Review units and cell references before copying the formula down a worksheet.

Common mistakes and limitations

  • Using the wrong base value.
  • Entering a percentage as 15 instead of 0.15 in Excel.
  • Confusing percentage points with percent change.

Important limitation: A percentage is a ratio, so the meaning depends on the chosen base. Division by zero is undefined.
