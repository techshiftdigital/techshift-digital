Time Zone Converter

Convert a date and time between world time zones using browser time-zone rules.

Enter your information and select the action above.

How it works

Enter a wall-clock time in the source zone. The converter applies time-zone and daylight-saving rules available in your browser.

Important limitations

Times skipped or repeated during daylight-saving changes can be ambiguous; the tool reports those cases rather than silently guessing.
