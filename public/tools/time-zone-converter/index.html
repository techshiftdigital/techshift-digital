What is the Time Zone Converter?

Convert a date and time between commonly used IANA time zones. Use it for a quick, transparent result, then review the method and boundaries below before relying on the answer.

How it works

The converter interprets the entered wall-clock time in the source zone and displays the equivalent instant in the destination zone.

The calculator keeps full precision during the calculation and rounds the displayed result for readability.

Practical example

09:00 in Islamabad (UTC+5) corresponds to 04:00 UTC when no offset change applies.

Common mistakes and limitations

  • Using fixed UTC offsets instead of named time zones.
  • Ignoring daylight-saving transitions.
  • Forgetting the converted date can be the previous or next day.

Important limitation: Time-zone offsets can change with daylight-saving rules and government decisions. Recheck future appointments near DST transitions.
