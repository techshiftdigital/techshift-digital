What is the Average, Median & Mode Calculator?

Calculate mean, median, mode, minimum, maximum and range from a list of numbers. Use it for a quick, transparent result, then review the method and boundaries below before relying on the answer.

Formula

Mean = sum ÷ count; median is the middle ordered value; mode is the most frequent value.

The calculator keeps full precision during the calculation and rounds the displayed result for readability.

Practical example

For 2, 3, 3, 7 and 10, the mean is 5, median is 3, mode is 3 and range is 8.

How to use this in Excel

Place the inputs in the cells referenced below, enter the formula, and apply the appropriate number format.

=AVERAGE(A2:A6), =MEDIAN(A2:A6), =MODE.SNGL(A2:A6)

Review units and cell references before copying the formula down a worksheet.

Common mistakes and limitations

  • Calculating the median before sorting.
  • Assuming average always means arithmetic mean.
  • Reporting one mode when several values tie.

Important limitation: A dataset can have no mode or multiple modes. The mean can be distorted by outliers.
