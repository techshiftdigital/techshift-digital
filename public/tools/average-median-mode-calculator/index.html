Average, Median & Mode Calculator

Calculate mean, median, mode, range and sum for a list of numbers.

Enter your values and select Calculate.

How it works

Mean is the sum divided by count; median is the middle sorted value; mode is the most frequent value.

Important limitations

Maximum 1,000 numbers. Mode may have more than one value or no unique mode.
