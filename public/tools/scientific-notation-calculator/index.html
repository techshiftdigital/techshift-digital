What is the Scientific Notation Calculator?

Convert decimal numbers to normalized scientific notation and back. Use it for a quick, transparent result, then review the method and boundaries below before relying on the answer.

Formula

Scientific notation writes a non-zero number as a × 10^n where 1 ≤ |a| < 10.

The calculator keeps full precision during the calculation and rounds the displayed result for readability.

Practical example

12,500,000 becomes 1.25 × 10^7; 3.2e-4 becomes 0.00032.

How to use this in Excel

Place the inputs in the cells referenced below, enter the formula, and apply the appropriate number format.

=TEXT(A2,"0.00E+00")

Review units and cell references before copying the formula down a worksheet.

Common mistakes and limitations

  • Moving the decimal in the wrong direction.
  • Forgetting that a negative exponent represents a small value.
  • Confusing e notation with a literal letter.

Important limitation: Displayed precision is rounded. Extremely large or small values are limited by browser floating-point range.
