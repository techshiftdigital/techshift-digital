Unix Timestamp Converter

Convert Unix timestamps to readable UTC dates and UTC dates back to Unix seconds or milliseconds.

Enter your information and select the action above.

How it works

Unix timestamps count elapsed time since 1970-01-01T00:00:00Z. Select seconds or milliseconds explicitly.

Important limitations

Dates are interpreted and displayed in UTC. Leap seconds are not separately represented.
