What is the Unix Timestamp Converter?

Convert Unix timestamps to readable UTC and local dates, or convert a date to Unix time. Use it for a quick, transparent result, then review the method and boundaries below before relying on the answer.

How it works

Unix time counts elapsed seconds since 1970-01-01 00:00:00 UTC, excluding leap seconds.

The calculator keeps full precision during the calculation and rounds the displayed result for readability.

Practical example

Unix timestamp 0 represents 1 January 1970 at 00:00:00 UTC.

How to use this in Excel

Place the inputs in the cells referenced below, enter the formula, and apply the appropriate number format.

Unix seconds to Excel date: =A2/86400+DATE(1970,1,1)

Review units and cell references before copying the formula down a worksheet.

Common mistakes and limitations

  • Confusing seconds and milliseconds.
  • Interpreting UTC as local time.
  • Assuming Unix time models leap seconds.

Important limitation: Some systems use milliseconds rather than seconds. The same instant displays differently across time zones.
