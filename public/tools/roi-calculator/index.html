What is the ROI Calculator?

Calculate return on investment from initial cost and final value. Use it for a quick, transparent result, then review the method and boundaries below before relying on the answer.

Formula

ROI = (final value − investment cost) ÷ investment cost × 100.

The calculator keeps full precision during the calculation and rounds the displayed result for readability.

Practical example

An investment costing $5,000 and later worth $6,250 has an ROI of 25%.

How to use this in Excel

Place the inputs in the cells referenced below, enter the formula, and apply the appropriate number format.

=(B2-A2)/A2

Review units and cell references before copying the formula down a worksheet.

Common mistakes and limitations

  • Ignoring fees and taxes.
  • Comparing ROIs over different time periods.
  • Using revenue instead of net gain.

Important limitation: Simple ROI ignores holding period, risk, inflation and the timing of cash flows. Compare annualized returns for investments held for different periods.
