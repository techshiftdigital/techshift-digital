ROI Calculator

Calculate investment return, net gain and ROI percentage from cost and final value.

Enter your values and select Calculate.

How it works

ROI = (proceeds − cost) ÷ cost × 100. A 1,000 investment returning 1,350 yields 35% ROI.

Important limitations

Does not annualize returns or account for risk, taxes or the time value of money.
