What is the Age Calculator?

Calculate age in completed years, months and days from a date of birth. Use it for a quick, transparent result, then review the method and boundaries below before relying on the answer.

How it works

Age is the calendar difference between the birth date and the comparison date, adjusted when the birthday has not yet occurred.

The calculator keeps full precision during the calculation and rounds the displayed result for readability.

Practical example

Someone born on 1 January 2000 is 26 years old on 1 January 2026.

How to use this in Excel

Place the inputs in the cells referenced below, enter the formula, and apply the appropriate number format.

=DATEDIF(A2,TODAY(),"y")

Review units and cell references before copying the formula down a worksheet.

Common mistakes and limitations

  • Subtracting years without checking whether the birthday has occurred.
  • Using total days divided by 365.
  • Entering a future birth date.

Important limitation: Results use calendar dates and do not account for time of birth. Leap-day birthdays may be treated differently for legal purposes.
