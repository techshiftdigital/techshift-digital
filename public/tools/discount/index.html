Discount calculator

Calculate a discount amount and the final price.

Enter your values, then select the action above.

How to calculate a discounted price

Multiply the original price by the discount percentage to find the saving. Subtract that saving from the original price. A 15% discount on 100 saves 15 and leaves a final price of 85.

Excel formula: =A2*(1-B2) when B2 contains a percentage such as 15%.

Important boundaries

This tool does not include tax, delivery charges or currency conversion. Discounts must be between 0% and 100%.
