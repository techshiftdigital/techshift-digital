What is the Fuel Cost Calculator?

Estimate fuel needed and trip cost from distance, efficiency and fuel price. Use it for a quick, transparent result, then review the method and boundaries below before relying on the answer.

Formula

Fuel used = distance ÷ fuel efficiency; trip cost = fuel used × price per litre.

The calculator keeps full precision during the calculation and rounds the displayed result for readability.

Practical example

A 500 km trip at 12 km/L uses 41.67 L. At $1.50/L, the estimated fuel cost is $62.50.

How to use this in Excel

Place the inputs in the cells referenced below, enter the formula, and apply the appropriate number format.

=(A2/B2)*C2

Review units and cell references before copying the formula down a worksheet.

Common mistakes and limitations

  • Mixing miles and kilometres.
  • Using L/100 km as though it were km/L.
  • Ignoring return distance.

Important limitation: Traffic, speed, load, weather, detours and vehicle condition can change actual consumption.
