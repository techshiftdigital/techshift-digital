What is the Calorie / BMR Calculator?

Estimate basal metabolic rate and daily calorie needs using the Mifflin–St Jeor equation. Use it for a quick, transparent result, then review the method and boundaries below before relying on the answer.

Formula

BMR for men = 10W + 6.25H − 5A + 5; for women = 10W + 6.25H − 5A − 161, with kg, cm and years.

The calculator keeps full precision during the calculation and rounds the displayed result for readability.

Practical example

A 30-year-old man weighing 75 kg and 175 cm tall has an estimated BMR of 1,699 kcal/day.

How to use this in Excel

Place the inputs in the cells referenced below, enter the formula, and apply the appropriate number format.

Male: =10*A2+6.25*B2-5*C2+5; Female: =10*A2+6.25*B2-5*C2-161

Review units and cell references before copying the formula down a worksheet.

Common mistakes and limitations

  • Confusing BMR with total daily energy expenditure.
  • Using pounds and inches without conversion.
  • Creating an aggressive calorie target without professional advice.

Important limitation: This is a population estimate, not a medical or nutrition prescription. Metabolism, activity and health conditions vary.
