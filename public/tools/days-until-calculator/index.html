What is the Days Until / Countdown Calculator?

Count the time remaining until a target date or the time elapsed since it. Use it for a quick, transparent result, then review the method and boundaries below before relying on the answer.

Formula

Calendar-day difference = target date − today, normalized to midnight to avoid partial-day drift.

The calculator keeps full precision during the calculation and rounds the displayed result for readability.

Practical example

If today is 1 December and the target is 25 December, the countdown is 24 elapsed days.

How to use this in Excel

Place the inputs in the cells referenced below, enter the formula, and apply the appropriate number format.

=A2-TODAY()

Review units and cell references before copying the formula down a worksheet.

Common mistakes and limitations

  • Mixing inclusive and exclusive counts.
  • Ignoring time-zone differences.
  • Assuming a negative value is an error rather than an elapsed date.

Important limitation: The count depends on time zone and whether the current day is included. Event times require more precision than date-only counting.
