What is the Loan / EMI Calculator?

Estimate a fixed monthly loan payment, total repayment and total interest. Use it for a quick, transparent result, then review the method and boundaries below before relying on the answer.

Formula

Payment = P × r × (1+r)^n ÷ ((1+r)^n − 1), where P is principal, r is the monthly rate and n is the number of payments.

The calculator keeps full precision during the calculation and rounds the displayed result for readability.

Practical example

A $100,000 loan at 6% for 10 years has an estimated monthly payment of about $1,110.21 before fees or insurance.

How to use this in Excel

Place the inputs in the cells referenced below, enter the formula, and apply the appropriate number format.

=PMT(B2/12,C2*12,-A2)

Review units and cell references before copying the formula down a worksheet.

Common mistakes and limitations

  • Using the annual interest rate as the monthly rate.
  • Mixing years and months.
  • Comparing loans on payment alone instead of total cost.

Important limitation: The estimate assumes a fixed rate and equal monthly payments. Fees, taxes, insurance, prepayments and rate changes are excluded.
