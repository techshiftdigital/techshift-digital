What is the Unit Price Calculator?

Compare products by cost per unit and identify the lower unit price. Use it for a quick, transparent result, then review the method and boundaries below before relying on the answer.

Formula

Unit price = total package price ÷ quantity or size.

The calculator keeps full precision during the calculation and rounds the displayed result for readability.

Practical example

A 750 g pack for $6 costs $0.008 per gram, while a 1,000 g pack for $7.50 costs $0.0075 per gram and is cheaper per gram.

How to use this in Excel

Place the inputs in the cells referenced below, enter the formula, and apply the appropriate number format.

=A2/B2

Review units and cell references before copying the formula down a worksheet.

Common mistakes and limitations

  • Comparing grams with kilograms without conversion.
  • Ignoring usable quantity or waste.
  • Choosing the lowest unit price when a larger package may expire.

Important limitation: Products must use the same unit and comparable quality. Promotions, waste and usage differences can change the best choice.
