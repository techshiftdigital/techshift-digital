What is the BMI Calculator?

Calculate adult body mass index from weight and height. Use it for a quick, transparent result, then review the method and boundaries below before relying on the answer.

Formula

BMI = weight in kilograms ÷ height in metres squared.

The calculator keeps full precision during the calculation and rounds the displayed result for readability.

Practical example

A person weighing 70 kg with a height of 1.75 m has a BMI of 22.86.

How to use this in Excel

Place the inputs in the cells referenced below, enter the formula, and apply the appropriate number format.

=A2/(B2^2) when A2 is kilograms and B2 is metres

Review units and cell references before copying the formula down a worksheet.

Common mistakes and limitations

  • Entering centimetres without converting to metres.
  • Using BMI as an individual medical diagnosis.
  • Comparing adult categories with children’s growth standards.

Important limitation: BMI is a screening measure, not a diagnosis. It does not directly measure body fat and may be unsuitable for children, pregnancy or some athletic body types.
