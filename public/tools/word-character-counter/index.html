What is the Word & Character Counter?

Count words, characters, characters without spaces, sentences and reading time. Use it for a quick, transparent result, then review the method and boundaries below before relying on the answer.

How it works

Words are counted as non-empty groups separated by whitespace. Estimated reading time uses 200 words per minute.

The calculator keeps full precision during the calculation and rounds the displayed result for readability.

Practical example

“TechShift makes work easier.” contains 4 words and 29 characters including spaces and punctuation.

How to use this in Excel

Place the inputs in the cells referenced below, enter the formula, and apply the appropriate number format.

=LEN(A2) for characters; =IF(TRIM(A2)="",0,LEN(TRIM(A2))-LEN(SUBSTITUTE(TRIM(A2)," ",""))+1) for simple word counts

Review units and cell references before copying the formula down a worksheet.

Common mistakes and limitations

  • Comparing results from tools that use different tokenization rules.
  • Forgetting that spaces and line breaks count as characters.
  • Treating estimated reading time as exact.

Important limitation: Hyphenated terms, URLs, emoji and languages without spaces may be counted differently by other editors.
