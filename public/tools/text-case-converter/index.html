What is the Text Case Converter?

Convert text to uppercase, lowercase, title case or sentence case. Use it for a quick, transparent result, then review the method and boundaries below before relying on the answer.

How it works

Case conversion applies the selected capitalization rule to the supplied Unicode text.

The calculator keeps full precision during the calculation and rounds the displayed result for readability.

Practical example

“techshift digital tools” becomes “Techshift Digital Tools” in title case.

How to use this in Excel

Place the inputs in the cells referenced below, enter the formula, and apply the appropriate number format.

=UPPER(A2), =LOWER(A2), or =PROPER(A2)

Review units and cell references before copying the formula down a worksheet.

Common mistakes and limitations

  • Using title case for sentences.
  • Assuming automatic case conversion understands proper nouns.
  • Overwriting original text without reviewing acronyms.

Important limitation: Automatic title case may capitalize short words differently from a specific editorial style guide, and names may require manual correction.
