What is the Fraction Calculator?

Add, subtract, multiply or divide two fractions and simplify the result. Use it for a quick, transparent result, then review the method and boundaries below before relying on the answer.

Formula

For addition, a/b + c/d = (ad + bc) ÷ bd. Other operations follow equivalent common-denominator or reciprocal rules.

The calculator keeps full precision during the calculation and rounds the displayed result for readability.

Practical example

1/2 + 1/3 = 5/6.

How to use this in Excel

Place the inputs in the cells referenced below, enter the formula, and apply the appropriate number format.

Fractions can be calculated directly: =(A2/B2)+(C2/D2)

Review units and cell references before copying the formula down a worksheet.

Common mistakes and limitations

  • Adding numerators and denominators separately.
  • Forgetting to invert the second fraction when dividing.
  • Leaving the answer unsimplified.

Important limitation: Denominators cannot be zero. Very large integers may exceed exact browser-number precision.
