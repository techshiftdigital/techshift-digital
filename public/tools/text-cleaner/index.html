What is the Text Cleaner?

Remove extra spaces, blank lines and unwanted formatting from pasted text. Use it for a quick, transparent result, then review the method and boundaries below before relying on the answer.

How it works

The cleaner normalizes line endings, trims each line, collapses repeated spaces and optionally removes blank lines.

The calculator keeps full precision during the calculation and rounds the displayed result for readability.

Practical example

“ Quarterly report ” becomes “Quarterly report” after trimming and whitespace normalization.

How to use this in Excel

Place the inputs in the cells referenced below, enter the formula, and apply the appropriate number format.

=TRIM(CLEAN(A2))

Review units and cell references before copying the formula down a worksheet.

Common mistakes and limitations

  • Cleaning code where indentation is meaningful.
  • Removing blank lines from content that needs paragraph separation.
  • Assuming CLEAN removes every Unicode control character.

Important limitation: Plain-text cleaning removes spacing noise but cannot reliably preserve rich-text formatting, tables or intentional indentation.
