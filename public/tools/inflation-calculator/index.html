What is the Inflation Calculator?

Estimate how inflation changes a price or purchasing power over time. Use it for a quick, transparent result, then review the method and boundaries below before relying on the answer.

Formula

Future cost = current cost × (1 + annual inflation rate)^years.

The calculator keeps full precision during the calculation and rounds the displayed result for readability.

Practical example

At 3% annual inflation, an item costing $1,000 today would cost about $1,159.27 in 5 years.

How to use this in Excel

Place the inputs in the cells referenced below, enter the formula, and apply the appropriate number format.

=FV(B2,C2,0,-A2)

Review units and cell references before copying the formula down a worksheet.

Common mistakes and limitations

  • Applying a total inflation rate as though it were annual.
  • Ignoring compounding.
  • Assuming headline inflation matches every household’s costs.

Important limitation: Actual inflation varies by year, country and spending category. A constant-rate projection is illustrative, not a forecast.
