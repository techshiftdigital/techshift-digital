What is the Tip Calculator?

Calculate a tip, total bill and equal amount per person. Use it for a quick, transparent result, then review the method and boundaries below before relying on the answer.

Formula

Tip = bill × tip rate; per-person amount = (bill + tip) ÷ number of people.

The calculator keeps full precision during the calculation and rounds the displayed result for readability.

Practical example

A $120 bill with a 15% tip totals $138; split among 3 people, each pays $46.

How to use this in Excel

Place the inputs in the cells referenced below, enter the formula, and apply the appropriate number format.

=(A2*(1+B2))/C2

Review units and cell references before copying the formula down a worksheet.

Common mistakes and limitations

  • Tipping on a total that already includes service.
  • Dividing the bill before adding the tip inconsistently.
  • Entering 15 instead of 15% in Excel.

Important limitation: Local service charges and taxes may already be included. Check the bill before adding another tip.
