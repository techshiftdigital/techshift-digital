What is the Margin vs Markup Calculator?

Calculate profit, gross margin, markup and selling price from cost and price. Use it for a quick, transparent result, then review the method and boundaries below before relying on the answer.

Formula

Margin = profit ÷ selling price; markup = profit ÷ cost; profit = selling price − cost.

The calculator keeps full precision during the calculation and rounds the displayed result for readability.

Practical example

A product costing $60 and selling for $100 has $40 profit, a 40% margin and a 66.67% markup.

How to use this in Excel

Place the inputs in the cells referenced below, enter the formula, and apply the appropriate number format.

Margin: =(B2-A2)/B2; Markup: =(B2-A2)/A2

Review units and cell references before copying the formula down a worksheet.

Common mistakes and limitations

  • Using margin and markup as interchangeable terms.
  • Dividing profit by cost when calculating margin.
  • Setting prices without accounting for indirect costs.

Important limitation: This is a gross calculation. Overheads, tax, discounts, returns and other costs can materially reduce net profit.
