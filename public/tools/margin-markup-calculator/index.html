Margin vs Markup Calculator

Calculate selling price, profit margin and markup from cost and price or a target percentage.

Enter your values and select calculate.

How this tool works

Margin = profit ÷ selling price. Markup = profit ÷ cost. If an item costs 80 and sells for 100, margin is 20% while markup is 25%.

Important limitations

Target margin must be below 100%. Calculations exclude taxes, shipping and other overhead unless included in your cost.
