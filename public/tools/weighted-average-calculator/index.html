What is the Weighted Average Calculator?

Calculate an average when each value contributes a different weight. Use it for a quick, transparent result, then review the method and boundaries below before relying on the answer.

Formula

Weighted average = Σ(value × weight) ÷ Σ(weight).

The calculator keeps full precision during the calculation and rounds the displayed result for readability.

Practical example

Scores of 80 and 90 weighted 40% and 60% produce 86.

How to use this in Excel

Place the inputs in the cells referenced below, enter the formula, and apply the appropriate number format.

=SUMPRODUCT(A2:A3,B2:B3)/SUM(B2:B3)

Review units and cell references before copying the formula down a worksheet.

Common mistakes and limitations

  • Averaging the values without weights.
  • Forgetting to divide by the sum of weights.
  • Mixing percentages and whole-number weights inconsistently.

Important limitation: Weights must describe the same basis. They do not have to total 100, but their sum cannot be zero.
